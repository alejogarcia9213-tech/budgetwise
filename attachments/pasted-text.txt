# PART 1 — PRODUCT IDENTITY

Application name:

**Budget Wise**

Product type:

Personal and family budgeting + financial planning application.

The objective is to let a normal person answer, within seconds:

* How much did I earn?
* How much am I allowed to spend?
* How much have I actually spent?
* How much am I saving?
* How much am I investing?
* How much am I paying toward debt?
* How much money remains available?
* What is my net worth?
* Is my financial situation improving?
* Am I spending according to my desired allocation?

The application should feel significantly more capable than a spreadsheet while remaining much easier to use than professional accounting software.

# PART 25 — COMPOUND INTEREST MATH

Do not approximate calculations using simplistic yearly multiplication.

Use periodic compounding.

Definitions:

```text
P = initial principal
PMT = recurring contribution
annualRate = annual nominal rate
m = number of contribution/compounding periods per year
r = annualRate / m
n = years × m
```

For contributions made at the END of each period:

```text
FV =
P × (1+r)^n
+
PMT × [((1+r)^n - 1) / r]
```

For contributions made at the BEGINNING:

```text
FV =
P × (1+r)^n
+
PMT × [((1+r)^n - 1) / r] × (1+r)
```

Handle:

```text
r = 0
```

separately to avoid division by zero.

For time or interest-rate calculations where no reliable closed formula works with all supported combinations, use numerical methods such as:

```text
binary search
```

with:

* defined bounds
* tolerance
* iteration limit
* impossible-case detection

Never produce NaN or Infinity in the UI.
